MODULE // 05 Strict Epistemic Stratification

Human trials, cohorts, mice, cells, and inference are never treated the same.

Most wellness sites cite a 50 µM cancer-cell dish assay or a forced-swim mouse paper as proof that an oral capsule works in patients. Pharmawiki enforces hard epistemic ceilings across five distinct tiers—automatically auditing allometric animal doses and unbound human plasma exposure (Cmax,u).

What stays local, and what does not

Pharmawiki has no search backend or analytics. To return live catalog matches, your search terms go directly from your browser to PubChem and RxNorm, which may process requests under their own policies. The protocol log is saved only in browser storage. Do not enter patient-identifying information.
