Federated Open Data Sources

MIT Open Source

Pharmapedia queries public scientific repositories directly from your browser with zero tracking middleware:

  • NCBI PubChem PUG-REST & PUG-View: 118M+ chemical structures, IUPAC properties, XLogP, TPSA, and toxicology/pharmacology summaries.
  • EBI ChEMBL v34: 2.4M+ bioactive molecules, clinical approval phases, ATC classes, and human target Ki / IC50 / EC50 assays.
  • US FDA OpenFDA Drug Labels: Official SPL prescribing monographs, CYP450 pharmacokinetics, boxed warnings, and drug-drug interactions.
  • NCBI PubMed E-Utilities: 37M+ primary biomedical publications stratified by study design (Grades A–E).
